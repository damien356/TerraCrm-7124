Purpose of this document: a complete, self-contained brief on what Terra Ops is, what already exists,
and every single thing still to be built. Written so it can be handed to another AI or developer cold, with no
prior conversation, and they can add detail or complexity to any section without breaking what's already
there.




  1. Executive summary
Terra Ops is one platform replacing ServiceM8 + ActiveCampaign + the phone system for a flooring
business. Website feeds leads in, Xero takes the accounting, Stripe takes payments, an AI agent writes in
the owner's voice, and installers work from a restricted mobile app that never shows them customer
pricing.

Roughly 45% of the intended platform is built and working. The core job system, dispatch, tiering,
availability, geo-tracking and the job-file media system are live. Still to build: a forms engine, financial
integrations, CRM and email campaigns, SMS/voice with an AI agent, materials ledger with barcode
scanning, and website lead capture.

Three hard constraints that must never be broken:

 1. Terra Flooring, Floors by You and JSL Energy are 100% separate businesses. Never combine,
    link, cross-reference or merge them. This spec is Terra Flooring only. Do not propose consolidation —
    the answer is permanently no.

 2. Installers must never see customer pricing, margins, quotes, invoices, supplier costs, or other
    installers' tasks and rates. Enforced server-side, not in the UI.
 3. Installers on a lower rating tier are never told a job went to a higher tier. They only ever see
    "Sorry, this job was already accepted."

  4. Data model — the locked-in decisions
These were decided deliberately and should be treated as fixed foundations. Add to them; don't rewrite
them.


Contacts and companies
    Contacts are people. They exist once, forever. No duplicate contact per job.

    Companies are an optional wrapper over contacts ( company_contacts join). A person can belong
    to a company or stand alone.
    Billing is set per job: jobs.billToType = "company" | "contact" .
    Jobs have many contacts with roles ( job_contacts ), each with their own flags: receivesSms ,
    receivesEmail , canApproveQuote , onSiteContact . So a builder, a tenant and a strata manager
    can all sit on one job with different permissions.


Jobs and tasks

    Tasks are the dispatch unit, not jobs. One job produces many dispatches. Each task carries its own
    skill, crew size, assigned installer and pay. A single job might be "screed Monday, lay Tuesday, cove
   Thursday" — three tasks, potentially three different installers.
   Job statuses, skills and rates are editable data in Settings, never hardcoded enums.


Installers and skills

   Skills are ticks on the installer card ( installer_skills ), each with a per-skill rate and a
    canLead flag.

   Tasks are only ever offered to installers ticked for that skill.
   Crew capacity per installer: solo / own_offsider / needs_partner .

   A furniture flag on a task forces crew size 2.
   Installers always see the customer's name and phone — except in tender mode.
   One pricing tier for customers.


Dispatch and tiering

   Dispatch is direct (to one installer) or broadcast (to everyone with the skill).
   Broadcast: first accept wins, all other offers auto-close to "filled" .

   Offers expire after 2 hours, then escalate to the owner.
    INSTANT_ACCEPT_MIN_STARS = 4 . Installers rated 3 stars and under get a provisional accept held
   for 2 hours, letting a higher-rated installer take it first.
   Losing installers only ever see "Sorry, this job was already accepted."


Auth and roles

   Roles: admin | installer .

   The first user ever to sign in is auto-promoted to admin; everyone after defaults to installer and must
   be linked to an installer record by an admin.
   Disabled accounts are refused at the middleware.
   Self-protection: an admin can't demote or disable their own login.

Rules in force:

    Photos, video up to 60 seconds, and PDFs in any bucket.
    Crew can add, never delete. The office archives instead of deleting, so the record survives a dispute.
    Cannot start a job until damage photos are added or "nothing found" is ticked — that tick is stamped
    with who and when.
    Cannot mark complete until completion photos meet the per-skill minimum (default 4, editable in
    Settings).
    Everything auto-stamped: uploader, timestamp, GPS, task, area. Nobody names a file.
    Anything dropped in found writes a site_finding activity entry against the job — the hook the
    variation form plugs into.
    Files go phone/browser straight to Tigris, never through the API. Links are signed on read; nothing
    sits on a public URL.

10. Admin-only estimating, price book & quote
automation
This section adds a fast on-site estimating system to the Terra Ops admin job card. It replaces the slow spreadsheet workflow while
preserving a reliable, auditable calculation engine. It is part of the core backlog and must be built without weakening the existing
server-side installer restrictions.

     Locked decision - AI assists retrieval, never arithmetic
     The AI bar may understand natural-language searches and synonyms, but every quantity, rate, pack calculation,
     supplier fee, trim cut, GST amount and margin is calculated deterministically from stored structured data. The AI
     must never invent a product, price or measurement.




10.1 Outcomes
 - An admin can build a complete estimate on site from one responsive section of the job card.

 - Common measurements are entered directly: m2, lineal metres, each, hours, bags, rolls, packs, boxes and custom units.

 - Commercial vinyl captures floor area, coving, internal and external corners, weld length, skim/levelling, stairs and other preparation.

 - Customer selling prices, installer rates, supplier costs and job-specific overrides remain separate.

 - Products and rooms stay attached to the correct work package so descriptions never mix carpet areas with vinyl or timber areas.

 - The completed estimate generates a branded quote draft that remains fully editable before sending.


10.2 Non-negotiable security
 - The estimator, supplier costs, selling prices, margins, quote editor and price book are admin-only and enforced server-side.

 - Installers receive only their own assigned pay line items and their own total pay.

 - No installer route or API response may include customer selling prices, margins, supplier costs or another installer's rates.

 - All price and margin overrides record admin, time, previous value, new value and reason.

10.4 One-screen estimating workflow
Location: Admin job card > Estimate & Quote. The page is designed for a tablet or phone on site and desktop in the office. It
autosaves as a draft.

 Step           Admin action                                 System response

 1              Search or browse a trade group               Returns exact price-book items, products and synonyms

 2              Create a work package                        Keeps product, installation, preparation and areas together

 3              Enter quantities                             Calculates labour, material usage, waste, packs and supplier fees

 4              Assign areas beneath that product            Builds a product-specific scope sentence

 5              Review cost and margin                       Uses highest eligible installer rate until one is selected

 6              Select/assign installer                      Recalculates actual planned margin using that installer's rates

 7              Generate quote                               Creates editable line items and scope wording without losing cost links


Recommended screen layout
 - Top: AI-assisted search bar with a manual group filter and recent/favourite items.

 - Left/upper panel: alphabetically ordered trade groups. Items inside a group use admin-defined manual order, not alphabetical order.

 - Centre: work-package cards containing product, areas, measurements, labour and preparation.

 - Right/lower sticky summary: customer subtotal, materials, supplier fees, protected labour, gross profit, gross margin and warnings.

 - Actions: duplicate package, reorder, split, archive, save draft, preview quote and generate quote.

Quick-entry behaviour
 - Each item has a quantity box beside it. Example: Carpet install - 34 LM; Weld - 18 LM; Internal corner - 7 each.

 - The unit is displayed beside the quantity and comes from the price-book item. Admin can override the unit only on a custom line.

 - Keyboard-first entry on desktop; large tap targets, numeric keyboard and minimal modal windows on mobile/tablet.

 - Autosave each edit. Show Saved, Saving and Offline/Pending states clearly.

 - Allow optional notes, measurement source, area and attachment against every estimate line.

AI-assisted search
 - Understands terms such as 'R12 vinyl', 'commercial safety vinyl', 'skim', 'smoothing compound', 'cove' and supplier/product aliases.

 - Example query: '2 mm R12 Armstrong' filters by thickness, slip rating, brand and product category.

 - Results display product, supplier, SKU, category, rating, dimensions/pack size, current cost and last-updated date.

 - If nothing matches, say 'No matching item in the price book'. Never manufacture a result.

Master versus job behaviour

 - Deleting an item with history means archiving it, never removing the historical record.

10.6 Installer rates, eligibility & margin protection
Installer pricing is not a duplicate customer price list. It is a private cost schedule connected to the installer's skills and the price-book
items they are permitted to perform.

Skills and visible rate groups
 - An installer has skill toggles such as Carpet, Sheet Vinyl, Vinyl Plank, Hard Floor, Timber, Preparation, Stairs and Commercial
   Coving.

 - Only applicable rate groups and items appear when editing that installer. Admin can add or remove individual item eligibility.

 - Preparation can be linked as a companion group for carpet, vinyl and hard-floor installers, while still allowing individual prep skills to
   be disabled.

 - Task eligibility continues to use installer_skills and canLead. Rate visibility does not grant a skill.

Quote-time protected labour

     Owner decision - highest eligible rate
     Before an installer is selected, the estimate uses the highest active rate among installers eligible for each labour
     line. This protects the quote from being profitable only when the cheapest installer is available. If no eligible
     installer has a rate, use the item's default budget cost and show a blocking 'No eligible installer rate' warning
     before the quote is sent.


Selecting an installer
 - The admin may view eligible installers with availability, skill match, rating tier, distance/travel estimate, relevant rate and forecast job
   margin.

 - The system may suggest alternatives, but must not recommend an installer solely because they are cheapest.

 - After selection, actual planned labour cost uses that installer's snapshotted rates and the margin recalculates instantly.

 - A later change to the installer's master rates does not alter already accepted/dispatched task pay.

Low-margin approval

     Owner decision - warn and approve
     If the selected installer or any estimate edit drops the job below the configured minimum gross margin, show a
     persistent red warning. The job may proceed only after an admin approves it and enters a reason. Do not
     automatically change the customer's price and do not block the installer from assignment.


Installer view

     Owner decision - own line items and total pay
     The installer sees their own pay line items, quantities, units, rates and calculated total for the assigned task, plus
     any approved pay variation. They never receive customer prices, profit/margin data, supplier costs or other
     installers' rates.


Margin calculation
 - Gross profit = customer revenue ex-GST minus material costs, supplier fees, installer/subcontract labour and other direct job costs.

 - Gross margin % = gross profit divided by customer revenue ex-GST.

 - Show both protected margin (highest eligible installer) and selected-installer margin when an installer has been chosen.

10.7 Product catalogue & supplier rules
The material selector and AI bar search one structured product catalogue. Product records can carry different attributes by category
without forcing every product into flooring-specific fields.

 Product field                              Examples / rule

 Identity                                   Supplier, brand, product name, supplier SKU, Terra alias

 Classification                             Carpet, carpet tile, sheet vinyl, safety vinyl, LVT, timber, adhesive, leveller, trim, moisture barrier

 Technical attributes                       Thickness, width, roll length, slip rating, colour, coverage, density, pack quantity, stock length

 Purchase data                              Cost, purchase unit, minimum order, pack/box multiple, waste factor, last verified date

 Quote data                                 Suggested sell price, sell unit, customer-facing name and description template


Supplier fee engine
 - Combine all selected products from the same supplier before evaluating order thresholds.

 - Apply a dispatch fee once per supplier order/job, not once per product line.

 - Show the fee separately in cost calculations. Whether it appears separately or is absorbed into selling prices is configurable.

 - Rules are effective-dated and may be flat, threshold-based, percentage, zone/postcode-based or manual.

     Armstrong seed rule supplied by owner
     If the Armstrong product subtotal is $1,000 or less, apply a $150 dispatch fee. If the subtotal is over $1,000, apply a
     $100 dispatch fee. The threshold, fee and tax basis remain editable configuration.


Material quantity calculations
 - Coverage products calculate required packs/bags/rolls from measured quantity, depth where relevant, coverage and waste.

 - Round purchases up to the supplier's purchase multiple while retaining the exact measured quantity for installation labour.

 - Show calculated requirement, purchasable quantity, waste/overage, cost and manual override.

 - A manually overridden purchase quantity is visibly flagged and requires a note when it is below the calculated requirement.

10.9 Trim measurement & cut optimisation
Trim products store stock length and pack quantity. After selecting a trim type, the admin enters every required door/opening size
beneath that exact trim product.

 Input                                      Rule

 Trim product                               Exact SKU/profile/colour; only identical stock may share offcuts

 Stock length                               Supplier value, e.g. 3.3 m

 Pack quantity                              Lengths per box; purchasing rounds up to full boxes where required

 Cuts                                       One row per doorway/opening: label, required length and quantity

 Saw kerf                                   Configurable; initial suggested default 3 mm

 Joining                                    Not allowed by default. May be enabled per trim/product if acceptable

 Waste allowance                            Configurable minimum plus actual optimisation waste


Calculation output
 - Use a deterministic one-dimensional cutting-stock calculation to minimise full lengths while accounting for saw kerf.

 - Return full lengths required, boxes required, total purchased length, used length, offcut/waste and total product cost.

 - Produce a cut list per physical length: for example Length 1 - Door A 920 mm, Door B 820 mm, Door C 760 mm.

 - Never combine offcuts across different SKU, profile, colour or job unless an admin deliberately adds known warehouse offcut stock
   later.

 - Allow the admin to override calculated lengths/boxes, but preserve the original calculation and flag the override.


10.10 Quote generation and editing
 - Generate quote sections in work-package order, with product, areas, installation, preparation, exclusions and optional notes.

 - Customer wording uses quote templates and structured area data. It must not expose supplier cost names or installer pay.

 - The admin can edit text, reorder lines, split/merge customer-facing sections, change quantities and change sell prices.

 - Editing wording alone does not alter calculations. Editing a linked quantity or price recalculates totals and margin.

 - The generated wording is a draft, not a locked template. Preserve the structured estimate behind the final edited text.

 - Quote revisions are versioned. Accepted versions remain reproducible exactly as sent.


10.11 Acceptance tests
 - Changing a master suggested price does not alter an existing job, quote or accepted task.

 - Creating a new job after the change uses the new master price-book version.

 - A job-specific price override never changes the master price book.

 - Two products on one job retain independent area lists and generate correct separate quote descriptions.

 - An R12 search returns only matching stored products and never invents a missing result.

 - Armstrong supplier totals trigger the correct single dispatch fee across multiple Armstrong products.

 - Trim optimisation respects 3.3 m stock lengths, kerf, pack multiples and SKU separation.

 - Highest eligible installer rate is used before assignment; selected rate replaces it for actual planned margin.

 - A below-minimum margin produces a red warning and cannot proceed without an admin approval reason.

12. Job lifecycle and Needs Attention engine
A job keeps its normal lifecycle stage while a separate rules engine calculates operational flags. A flag does not overwrite the job
status. This prevents the schedule and reports being corrupted just to show that somebody needs to act.


12.1 Flag behaviour
 - Red means overdue, blocked or likely to cost money. Amber means due soon. Blue/grey means legitimately waiting and has a next
   review date.

 - Every flag has a rule, severity, owner, due time, reason, clear next action and link to the exact record.

 - The system removes a flag automatically when its condition is resolved. Manual completion remains audited.

 - Snooze requires a reason and a new date. Indefinite snooze is not allowed.

 - Timers use configured Terra Flooring business hours and public holidays, not raw elapsed hours.

13. CRM, email and marketing automation

13.1 Unified customer timeline
 - The contact timeline combines permitted calls, SMS, emails, website enquiries, site visits, quotes, jobs, payments/invoices,
   complaints, defects, marketing events and internal notes.

 - Conversation categories: General enquiry, New work/quote request, Existing job, Accounts/payment, Complaint/defect, Supplier,
   Installer, Spam and Unclassified.

 - Conversation statuses: Unassigned, Assigned, Waiting on us, Waiting on customer, Resolved and Archived.

 - A recognised customer's unrelated question becomes a General enquiry on the contact timeline and in the team Inbox. It does not
   create or attach to a job.

 - General enquiries waiting on Terra turn amber after 2 working hours and red at end of day. A one-click action can later create a

16. Integration failure and audit system
Every external action is treated as a tracked delivery, not assumed successful. A failed email, SMS, payment, accounting push, file
upload or webhook must be visible and recoverable.


16.1 Integration event model
 Field                                      Required behaviour

 Provider and operation                     Zoho send/sync, SMS, voice, Stripe, Xero, storage, website webhook or other named action

 Internal/external IDs                      Link the Terra record and provider request/message/payment ID for de-duplication

 State                                      Queued, processing, succeeded, retrying, failed, cancelled or needs human action

 Attempts                                   Attempt count, last attempt, next retry, safe error summary and technical detail

 Ownership                                  Assigned staff member, severity, due time and linked Needs Attention flag

 Recovery                                   Idempotent retry where safe; manual resolve/relink/cancel with required reason

 - Automatic retry uses capped backoff and never creates duplicate emails, payments, invoices, leads or files.

 - After retry limits, create a red Needs Attention item. Do not leave failures buried in logs.

 - Do not show provider secrets or sensitive payloads in the UI, notifications or ordinary logs.


16.2 Immutable audit
 - Audit actor, time, action, entity, record ID, previous value, new value, reason, source surface and request/correlation ID.

 - High-risk events include login/security changes, permissions, prices/rates, margin approvals, quote/invoice changes, refunds, exports,
   deletions, integration configuration and AI/voice actions.

 - Job activity remains a readable operational timeline; the global audit view is the immutable security and change record.

 - Owners can filter/export audit data, but ordinary users cannot edit or delete it.
