# Quoting, rebuilt

What you said, in order: the quote screen makes no sense, you cannot search the
product list, and there is no labour on it at all. Then the bigger one, the
thing that actually saves you time: you want to tick rooms and pick a product
and have the quote write itself.

Five pieces. Delivered in this order, because each one leans on the one before.

---

## 1. Search that works, and labour on the quote

**The problem.** The product picker is a native dropdown. A native dropdown
only jumps to whatever starts with the last key you pressed, which is why it
feels like you can only search the first digit. And labour is nowhere. The rate
book has 84 priced work items sitting in it, across carpet, resilient, timber,
prep, demolition, trades, and none of it reaches a quote. You are typing
labour in by hand and pricing it out of your head.

**The fix.**

- The product picker becomes a real search box. Type "merida" and you get it.
  Type "andes" and you get the range. Type a supplier and you get their list.
- A labour picker next to it, running off the rate book.
- Category toggles across the top: CARPET, RESILIENT, TIMBER, PREP, REMOVAL,
  TRADES, OTHER. Click CARPET and every carpet rate comes up, the broadloom
  metre rates, the stair rates, the uplift. Click nothing and search instead,
  type "furniture" and you get Furniture shift out of the other category. Both
  ways into the same list.
- Once the job has a category on it, that toggle is already selected when the
  screen opens. Carpet job, carpet rates, no clicking.
- Labour sells at cost times 1.911, the same overhead, inefficiency and profit
  chain the materials already use. Your cost stays on your side of the screen.

## 2. Who lays it

**The rule you gave me.** Price it at the standard rate. An installer who
charges more than standard does not appear.

So under each labour line you get the layers who can actually do that work,
which is the skill tick list doing its job, and only the ones at or under
Terra's standard number. The dearer ones are counted and named, but they are
not in the pick list and they cannot be chosen by accident. Pick a layer and
the line prices at the standard rate regardless, so your margin does not move
depending on who you had in mind.

Nothing gets booked at this stage. A quote is not a job.

## 3. Broadcast for an in-between date

From the quote, before it is sold. You pick a window, Terra shows you who is
free across it, and you send it out. Real offers, real messages, exactly as you
said, because you would rather know who is available than guess.

This reuses the offer broadcast that already exists on the board, so a reply
lands in the same place replies already land.

## 4. The room composer

The big one.

You tick rooms. Four bedrooms, hallway, WIRs, cupboards, rumpus, lounge,
dining, attic, stairs. You pick the carpet, Andes Peak, Merida. You pick the
underlay, 10mm green foam. You tick that you are taking the old carpet away.

Terra writes the quote:

> Supply and install Andes Peak solution dyed nylon in Merida to master
> bedroom, bedrooms 2, 3 and 4, hallway, walk in robes, rumpus, lounge and
> dining. Includes 10mm green foam underlay, and uplift and disposal of the
> existing carpet.

And it writes the labour lines underneath it off the same ticks. Stairs ticked
means stair rates, by the step. Carpet removal ticked means the uplift rate
against the same metreage. You are not pricing disposal out of your head any
more, it comes off your own rate book.

The room ticks come from the measure, so this wants to be the same list you use
on site in the app. That is the next thing I need off you: your actual room
list, in your words.

## 5. The quote screen itself

Once the above is in, the screen gets rebuilt around it rather than being a
spreadsheet with eight columns. Supply, labour and the customer-facing wording
in three clear blocks, your cost and margin off to the side where it already
lives.

---

## Decisions already locked

- Labour marks up on the same chain as materials, cost x 1.911.
- Standard rate always. A dearer installer is excluded, not surcharged.
- Picking a layer on a quote does not book him.
- Broadcast from a quote sends real messages.
- Cost and margin never leave the admin screen. Installers see their own rate
  and nothing else, customers see a sell price.

## Still needed from you

- Your room list, in the words you use on site.
- Whether the customer-facing wording should break supply and install into two
  lines or read as one supply-and-install price per area.
